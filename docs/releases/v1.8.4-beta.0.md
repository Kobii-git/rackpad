# Rackpad v1.8.4-beta.0

This beta focuses on the rear cabling and detailed rack feedback in
[issue #139](https://github.com/Kobii-git/rackpad/issues/139). Studio remains
opt-in, and the classic rack view remains available. Docker is the supported
general deployment; native Proxmox LXC remains experimental.

## What changed

- Same-face rear cables use direct in-rack curves across the available rack
  height, including shelves and intermediate equipment. Existing front patch
  cords retain their direct routing.
- Each cable saves an Automatic, Direct, Managed or Manual routing mode.
  Smooth/Orthogonal remains a separate personal drawing preference.
- Managed routes can pass through ordered guide positions on installed brush
  panels or cable managers. Guides follow device movement and rotation. A
  front/rear passage remains one cable, with no added ports or trace hops.
- Mixed-face continuations identify the destination device, port and face.
  Endpoint actions reveal the other end while retaining cable selection.
- Compact presentation uses quieter rails, smaller labels, thin cables and
  side-by-side rack elevations. Hardware starters include category-specific
  details, with matching artwork and routing in SVG/PNG exports.
- Existing ports, cables, placement and device-owned layout snapshots are
  preserved. Revised template geometry requires preview and explicit apply.

## Upgrade and recovery

Use the immutable image `ghcr.io/kobii-git/rackpad:1.8.4-beta.0` once its
publication is complete. The moving `beta` image follows the beta branch;
`latest` remains the stable channel.

This release advances the database from schema 51 to **52**. Existing links
with manual waypoints become Manual; other links become Automatic. Both inactive
guides and waypoints survive routing-mode changes. Guide references are removed
transactionally when a guide device is deleted or leaves its declared room;
the cable and its connectivity remain intact.

Before upgrading, keep a compatible pre-upgrade database or volume backup,
the previous image, configuration and original `RACKPAD_SECRET_KEY` together.
Migrations are forward-only. An older binary must not open schema-52 data;
rollback requires restoring the matching pre-upgrade application and backup.
Retain the [earlier security upgrade guidance](v1.8.3.md#upgrade-and-recovery)
when upgrading from older releases.

## Tester acceptance

Please test the published beta on the arrangement reported in #139:

1. Rear switch-to-server links spanning 2U, 4U and 6U stay inside the rack,
   including paths over shelves. Front patch cords remain readable.
2. Front, Rear and Both views handle mixed-face connections, continuation
   labels, selection, tracing and opposite-endpoint focus correctly.
3. Routing modes and brush guides survive save/reload and backup/restore.
   Moving a guide follows the device; deleting it preserves the cable.
4. Rotated, side-mounted and rack-top equipment keeps exact port endpoints.
   Cross-rack and cross-room handoffs remain understandable.
5. Compact viewing and SVG/PNG exports are readable, and custom template
   bindings and device layout snapshots retain their connections.

Include the tested version, browser/platform and screenshots of anything that
still looks wrong. The synthetic rack is an approximation of the supplied
images; automated checks do not establish acceptance on the actual equipment.
Issue #139 stays open until that confirmation arrives.
